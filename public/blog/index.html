Why we write these posts

Most of what we publish here comes from real, recurring account questions — an M-Pesa deposit that hasn't shown up, a withdrawal that's taking longer than expected, or a login that suddenly stops working. Rather than generic troubleshooting copy, each post walks through the specific checks that actually resolve these issues, in the order that tends to work, and tells you exactly what to send support if the checks don't fix it — no vague "try again later" advice.
