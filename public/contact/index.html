When you message support

To help sort things out quickly on the first reply, please include:

  • The phone number or username on your DBBet account.
  • What happened, and when (date and time).
  • For payment issues: the M-Pesa transaction code and amount, and whether it's a deposit or a withdrawal.
  • For login issues: whether you're using the app or a mobile browser, and what error message (if any) you're seeing.
  • For a bonus query: which offer, and the date you registered or deposited.

The more specific detail you give up front, the fewer back-and-forth messages it takes to resolve — a vague "my deposit isn't working" message usually gets a first reply asking for exactly the details listed above, which just adds a delay you can skip.

Never share your password with anyone. DBBet staff will never ask for your full password or M-Pesa PIN.
