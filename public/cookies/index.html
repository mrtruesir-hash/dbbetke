Cookie notice

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help a website work and remember your preferences. This notice explains how DBBet uses them, the categories we set, and how to control them in your own browser.

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Session vs persistent cookies

A session cookie is deleted automatically when you close your browser — it exists only for that visit, and is typically what keeps you logged in during a single session. A persistent cookie stays on your device for a set period after you leave, which is how preference cookies remember your settings the next time you visit without you having to reconfigure them. Strictly necessary cookies may be either type depending on what they do.

Types of cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to work, keep you logged in and keep your account secure. These can't be switched off.
  • Preferences — remember choices such as language and display settings.
  • Analytics — help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These are anonymised where possible.
  • Marketing — used to measure campaigns and, where you agree, show more relevant offers. These are only set with your consent.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies in your browser settings — including deleting existing cookies and blocking new ones. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works, and strictly necessary cookies in particular are required to keep you logged in and your account secure.

  • Chrome (Android/desktop): Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Safari (iPhone): Settings app → Safari → Advanced → Website Data, or Privacy & Security → Block All Cookies.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.

These settings let you view, delete or block cookies site-by-site rather than all at once, so you can keep DBBet's necessary cookies while blocking marketing cookies elsewhere, for example.

First-party vs third-party cookies

A first-party cookie is set directly by dbbetke.com itself — for example, to remember you're logged in. A third-party cookie is set by a different domain embedded on the page, such as an analytics or payment provider. Browsers increasingly restrict third-party cookies by default, which is part of why analytics and marketing cookies on this notice are described as consent-based rather than automatic.

Third parties

Some cookies are set by trusted third parties, such as analytics and payment providers. Their use of data is covered by their own policies. See our privacy policy for more on how we handle data.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to accept cookies to use DBBet Kenya?
Strictly necessary cookies can't be switched off, since they keep you logged in and your account secure. Preferences, analytics and marketing cookies are optional and only set with consent where required.
Will blocking cookies break the site?
Blocking strictly necessary cookies will likely stop you staying logged in. Blocking preferences, analytics or marketing cookies shouldn't affect core functionality like betting or casino play.
Does this cookie notice cover DBBet's own platform too?
This notice covers dbbetke.com. DBBet's own platform, where you actually register, deposit and play, has its own cookie and privacy terms — check those directly on that platform.
What's the difference between a session and a persistent cookie?
A session cookie is deleted when you close your browser. A persistent cookie stays on your device for a set period so your preferences are remembered on your next visit.

Do Not Track and browser signals

Some browsers let you send a "Do Not Track" signal to websites. There's no single industry standard for how sites must respond to it, so like many sites, DBBet Kenya's cookie behavior is governed by the categories above and your explicit consent choices rather than by Do Not Track signals specifically. Blocking or clearing cookies directly in your browser settings, as described above, is the more reliable way to control what's stored than relying on a Do Not Track header.
